Portable Design System

Variable fonts. Fluid spacing.
Swappable color.

One CSS file. Every ratio, breakpoint, and spacing step is deliberately off the Tailwind/Bootstrap defaults — nothing here lines up with what a trained eye recognizes as templated. Works in WordPress, plain HTML, or anything else that accepts a stylesheet.
